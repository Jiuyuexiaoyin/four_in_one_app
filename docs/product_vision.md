# Product Vision

This app is not a simple habit, todo, or timer demo. The current app is only an Alpha MVP skeleton, and the simple Habits, Goals, Focus, and Review pages must not be treated as final product quality.

The product direction is a four-in-one productivity growth app inspired by:

- DayMark: goal, project, subproject structure and progress tracking.
- OffScreen: premium minimal UI, digital wellbeing, and screen behavior awareness.
- Tomato ToDo: focus execution, timer reliability, and strong focus modes.
- HabitDone: lightweight habit tracking, theme customization, and low-friction daily UI.

## Core Direction

- Long-term goals.
- Daily habits.
- Focus execution.
- Review and analytics.
- Digital wellbeing.
- UI customization.

## Product Principle

Each feature should eventually connect to the same growth loop: plan what matters, practice daily, execute with focus, review honestly, and tune the environment around attention.
