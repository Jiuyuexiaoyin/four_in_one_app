# Roadmap

This roadmap must be read together with `docs/product_vision.md`, `docs/ui_direction.md`, and `docs/source_ui_reference.md`. The app should move from functional MVP slices toward a unified four-in-one product inspired by DayMark, HabitDone, OffScreen, and Tomato ToDo.

## Phase A: Stabilize MVP And Phone Testing

- Keep the current MVP reliable on real Android devices.
- Fix bounded Alpha blockers.
- Preserve small, testable increments.

## Phase B: Theme Color Customization And Product Visual Upgrade

- Add theme color customization.
- Upgrade visual hierarchy and product polish.
- Keep the UI lightweight, premium, and restrained.
- Align visual decisions with HabitDone customization friendliness and OffScreen restraint.
- Avoid generic Flutter MVP surfaces.

## Phase C: Richer Habits

- Add proof, notes, skip, makeup, and multiple check-ins.
- Keep daily habit entry low-friction.
- Improve habit card identity, reminder editing, check-in clarity, recent activity, and heatmap readability.
- Continue HabitDone-inspired habit tracking without turning Habits into a todo list.

## Phase D: Goals Become Plan System

- Evolve Goals into a plan system.
- Support goal, project, subproject, and task hierarchy.
- Add meaningful progress tracking.
- Add clearer DayMark-like hierarchy affordance, color/icon identity, focused structure, and later history/stat views.
- Keep task/action as the executable unit and avoid direct goal-as-checkbox behavior.

## Phase E: Richer Focus Modes

- Expand focus modes beyond the basic timer.
- Keep timer reliability and restore behavior strong.
- Avoid deep focus features until the base loop is stable.
- Move toward Tomato ToDo-style execution: task-linked focus, pomodoro mode, count-up/down, strict mode, interruption records, and weekly focus overview.
- Do not add platform-heavy focus restrictions until permissions and user trust are clear.

## Phase F: Review Becomes Real Analytics

- Build real review and analytics from actual app data.
- Avoid fake trends or dashboard-heavy presentation.
- Move toward OffScreen-like calm data visuals: large key metrics, soft charts, timelines, heatmap-like summaries, and honest trend surfaces.
- Do not show analytics that the app cannot support with real stored data.

## Phase G: Digital Wellbeing And Screen Behavior

- Add digital wellbeing and screen behavior awareness.
- Keep privacy, platform limits, and user control explicit.
- Use OffScreen as the tone reference for screen behavior awareness: calm, private, explanatory, and not punitive.
- Keep digital wellbeing separate from focus blocking until the product rationale is clear.
